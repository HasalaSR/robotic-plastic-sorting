About the Research

This research investigates whether accurate artificial-intelligence-based object detection can be translated into reliable physical recovery when integrated with an industrial robotic sorting system operating on a moving conveyor.

Rather than evaluating computer vision in isolation, the work examines the complete perception-to-action chain using real post-consumer plastic waste. The experimental platform integrates image acquisition, AI-based detection, object selection, conveyor tracking, robot motion and pneumatic grasping into a single end-to-end system.

Commissioned robotic sorting platform prepared for experimental trials with real post-consumer plastic samples.

Key Results

High perception performance did not automatically translate into equally high physical recovery. Reliable deployment depended on the entire chain from seeing and selecting an object to tracking, grasping and physically recovering it.

Key Takeaway

Deployment readiness is a chain property.

High-performing AI is necessary, but reliable robotic sorting depends on perception, tracking, target selection, timing, grasping and physical recovery operating effectively as one coordinated system.
