Research Project

AI Can See the Plastic. But Can the Robot Pick It?

End-to-end evaluation of deep-learning perception and industrial robotic manipulation for sorting real post-consumer plastic waste.

About System Key Results Video Research Outputs Team

About the Research

This research investigates how accurately artificial-intelligence-based object detection translates into successful physical sorting when integrated with an industrial delta robot operating on a moving conveyor.

The system was developed using real post-consumer waste and evaluates the complete perception-to-action pipeline rather than considering computer-vision accuracy alone.

Key Results

High perception accuracy did not automatically produce high physical recovery. Mechanical execution, grasping and system timing remained critical determinants of overall performance.

20,100 Annotated images used in the experimental dataset
0.601 YOLOv11m validation mAP@0.5
0.88 m/s Experimentally validated conveyor speed
22.7–44.0% Robotic recovery across the five evaluated target categories

Key Takeaway

Deployment readiness is a chain property.

Reliable robotic sorting requires perception, tracking, timing, grasping and manipulation to perform effectively as one integrated system.
